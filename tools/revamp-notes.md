| Hero | a 2D smoke tunnel drawn on a canvas, after the wind-tunnel photographs of smoke lines over a car. The air is a real incompressible fluid on a staggered grid: semi-Lagrangian advection with a MacCormack correction, then a pressure projection. A padded hull around the name and the lede, faired to a blunt nose upstream, is a solid body the air cannot pass through (it slips along the surface; there is no viscosity), so the flow stays attached over the text and breaks up behind it. Every swirl is the body's own shear layer rolling up; nothing is injected. Smoke lines are released only at the left edge, drawn in ink, and fade with age. The canvas runs 110vh past the hero (40vh on phones, which have no pointer), so smoke pushed downward carries well into the page instead of stopping at an edge. A moving pointer pushes the air near it. Tunables are the constants at the top of `smoke.js`: `U0` speed, `CELLS` grid size, `LINE_GAP` line spacing, `NOSE_K` how far the nose is faired, `LIFE0`/`LIFE1` how long the smoke lasts, `PTR_K`/`PTR_R` the pointer |

It uses only the four page files at the root plus `assets/` and `fonts/`. No build step, no framework, no third-party script.

## Preview locally

```sh
cd lucavendruscolo.github.io
python -m http.server 8080
```

Then open http://localhost:8080/. Paths are absolute (`/assets/...`, `/fonts/...`), so a server at the repo root is required; opening the file directly will not load fonts or media.

After pushing, the page is at https://lucavendruscolo.github.io/.

## Things to try

- Move the pointer across the hero: the air follows it, folds and rolls up, then washes downstream. A still pointer does nothing.
- Press "Pause the smoke" (it persists across reloads).
- Turn on the OS "reduce motion" setting and reload: a pre-rendered still replaces the canvas and videos show native controls.
- Disable JavaScript: the page is complete and readable.
- Click a video to pause or play it. Tab through the page: skip link, nav, pause button, every video (Space or Enter toggles it) and link, in order.

## Editing copy

`content/content.json` holds the sentences and CV rows the page transcribes. Edit a sentence there, then mirror it in `index.html`. Keep the copy rules: first person, no em dashes, no buzzwords.
